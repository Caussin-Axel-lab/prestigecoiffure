Z-INDEX ANALYSIS:
-----------------

Current values in style.css:
- body::before (grain): z-index: 9999 [PROBLEMATIC - TOO HIGH]
- #scroll-progress: z-index: 200
- header.topnav: z-index: 100
- .mobile-menu: z-index: 99
- .hero-text: z-index: 2
- .signature-content: z-index: 3

STACKING CONTEXT HIERARCHY (lowest to highest):
1. Default (0)
2. .hero-text (2)
3. .signature-content (3)
4. .mobile-menu (99)
5. header.topnav (100)
6. #scroll-progress (200)
7. body::before grain (9999) ← EXCESSIVE

ISSUES:
-------
1. body::before at z-index: 9999 is WAY too high
   - Spans entire viewport with pointer-events: none (technically safe)
   - But it's excessive and could interfere with fixed/absolute elements
   - Recommended: 1-10 (above default, below nav)
   
2. Grain with pointer-events: none is technically correct
   - Won't block interaction
   - But 9999 suggests developer misunderstanding of z-index
   
3. scroll-progress at z-index: 200 is reasonable
   - Above nav (100) so it's visible
   - Appropriate for progress indicator
